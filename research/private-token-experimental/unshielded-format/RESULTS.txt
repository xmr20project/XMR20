6. DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENT (R-F) — HOW IT IS MET AND WHAT REMAINS
   Met by construction: public ownership is a derived decaf377-rdsa key (keyed BLAKE2b from the root, domain "xmr20/public-token/owner-key"),
   algebraically unrelated to any Monero key; records carry note ids (hashes), asset ids, amounts, owner keys and Penumbra public data only;
   migration claims use the plain Monero signature under the old output key (no key image); carriers are ordinary 1-in/2-out RingCT
   transactions built by monero-oxide with unchanged gamma decoy selection (the record only sets tx_extra; its size is the only change to
   the transaction shape). Verified by the audit in 3 and by the browser suite's network audit (no key image or carrier blob to the backend).
   Residual (not zero privacy impact): public amounts and owner keys in unshielded mode; the fee payer, timing and record sizes of every
   carrier; the two carriers of a private transfer share an operation id (linkable to each other); shield/unshield publish amounts and link
   the owner key to a shielded event; the claim links P to the unified address; historical XN1 disclosures stay public.
