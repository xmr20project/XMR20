**Help us lock the keys for XMR20 private tokens — 30 minutes, no crypto knowledge needed**

XMR20 is adding private token transfers on Monero. Private transfers are checked with zero-knowledge proofs, and proofs need a set
of public "parameter" files that every wallet uses.

**The problem.** Making those files creates a secret as a by-product. Anyone who kept that secret could print fake tokens forever,
and nobody could ever detect it. If our team made the files alone, you would have to trust us that we deleted the secret. We do
not think you should have to.

**The fix.** The files are made by a chain of people. Each person adds their own random secret and then throws it away. The
result is safe as long as at least ONE person in the chain really deleted theirs. The more unrelated people take part, the less
anyone has to trust any single one of them, including us. This is how Zcash, Penumbra, RISC Zero and others made their keys.

**What you would do (once, about 30 minutes, mostly waiting):**
1. Paste one line into your terminal (Mac or Linux). It downloads our small client (about 7 MB).
2. Run `xmr20-ceremony contribute` and type a long random sentence when asked. The client joins the queue, downloads the current
   state (about 60 MB), does the maths for 15 to 30 minutes, and uploads your result (about 60 MB).
3. It prints two short hashes. Post them on your own X, GitHub or Mastodon with the words "XMR20 ceremony" so nobody, including
   us, can later swap your step out.
4. Reboot your machine and delete the folder. Keep the hashes.

You never touch funds, keys or wallets. Nothing keeps running afterwards. You are not vouching for XMR20; you are only adding
randomness and deleting it.

**If you are technical:** please build the client from source instead of using the installer (`git clone <repo> && cargo build
--release`, about 10 minutes). Participants who build from source are the ones that make the result trustworthy, because they do
not rely on any code we served. We would love at least a few of you.

**How you check us afterwards.** When the ceremony closes we publish every contribution and the final files. Anyone can run
`xmr20-ceremony verify` from a fresh checkout: it rebuilds the circuits from source, replays every step and confirms the published
keys match. Your hashes will be in the published record.
