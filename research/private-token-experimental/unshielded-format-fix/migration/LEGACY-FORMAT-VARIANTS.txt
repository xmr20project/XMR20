RULES FOR THE MIGRATION PATH (CONFORMANCE 6):
  - The Solana chain label inside the record ('mainnet' or 'mainnet-beta') and the record's fee field (150 or 200 bps at launch) are
    informational for migration: the asset identity is the Solana MINT (record.mint == catalog mint for every sample), the amount is
    the holder's legacy balance from the legacy indexer at the cutoff height, and the owner is the legacy output key. Nothing in
    the private path parses the launch JSON; the production legacy indexer, which already indexes every version, exports the rows.
  - Tokens without a Solana bridge program (12 of 66) migrate the same way; they simply have no Solana redemption path.
  - Per-token transfer tax (150 or 0 bps) is enforced by the custody program from the mint's own transfer-fee extension on every
    deposit and redemption (tested with the real mint bytes of five tokens); migration never applies it.
  - Future launches: any new inscription version must be added to the LEGACY INDEXER's parser; the migration path is unchanged
    as long as it receives (mint, legacy owner key, amount) rows. The catalog is the list of mints; nothing is per-ticker
    (tickers repeat: MCAT, TEST, QTEST, XMR, XMR20 appear more than once).
