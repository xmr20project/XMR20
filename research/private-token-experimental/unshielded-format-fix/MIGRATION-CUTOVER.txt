MIGRATION OF EXISTING MONERO-SIDE XMR20 HOLDINGS INTO THE PUBLIC FORMAT — PROCEDURE AND WHAT WAS TESTED
research/token-format-unshielded-20261025 — 25 October 2026. Tested with SYNTHETIC snapshots only. No live cutoff was chosen,
no real holding was migrated or frozen, no historical disclosure can be erased. Independent review: NOT performed.

1. ENTITLEMENT AND TARGET
  Every legacy lot (mint, legacy one-time output key P, amount at the cutoff) migrates ONCE into exactly `amount` of the same asset
  (asset id derived from the Solana mint, identical to the shielded pool's) as ONE PUBLIC NOTE owned by the claimant's owner key.
  No fee, no bridge tax, no Solana round trip; the credit carrier's XMR fee is paid by the issuer and disclosed. Shielding afterwards
  is the holder's own explicit action (1 transaction, 1 fee). Tested: 492,500,000 synthetic X lot -> exactly 492,500,000 public X
  (flow.test.mjs step 7); the release candidate's catalog suite (66 mints incl. EXTREMIST 10,000 at 6 decimals) is the model for the
  per-mint catalog and is reused unchanged in migration/ (snapshot-reconcile.mjs, third-party-assets.json).
2. CLAIM (unchanged from the rehearsed path; ONLY the credit target changed)
  The holder signs {profile, manifest id, MIGRATE, claim id, amount, owner = unified token address xmr20t1…} with the plain Monero
  signature under P (R = rG + cP; no key image, no ring position, no native spend, no unspentness proof). The registry verifies the
  signature against the snapshot row and consumes the claim id; the issuer publishes XC1 kind 2 bound to the claim id. The indexer
  refuses any second credit for that binding (duplicate claim, replayed claim, fresh installation of the same phrase: all refused —
  tested in the release candidate's suites and in flow.test.mjs). Keys: only the holder's Monero keys sign; nothing leaves the worker.
3. WHAT THE CLAIM PUBLISHES (disclosure, honest statement)
  The claim links the legacy output key P to the claimant's unified address (owner key + shielded address). That link is public at
  claim time (as in the earlier candidates: DISCLOSURES "migration disclosure"). It adds NO native key image or output/image relation.
  Historical XN1 disclosures (P-to-image proofs already on chain) remain public forever; migration cannot undo them.
4. STATES AND DOUBLE-CREDIT PREVENTION
  legacy right (snapshot row) -> consumed by exactly one XC1 credit -> public note -> (XS1) shielded note -> (XU1) public note -> (XR1) burn.
  Buckets reconciled by the operator: unclaimed legacy rights + credits awaiting indexing + public supply + shielded supply + pending
  payouts == reserve (operator.liabilities; flow.test.mjs asserts ok after migration and after payout). The indexer's own invariant:
  publicSupply + shieldedSupply == minted - burned per asset, checked after every block in the suites.
5. PRODUCTION SNAPSHOT (to be executed by the owner's operation; not run here)
  a. Choose the cutoff height H and its block hash on the LEGACY indexer (the production legacy indexer already parses every inscription
     version; nothing in this candidate parses launch JSON). Publish H in advance; stop accepting legacy MOVE/BURN after H in every
     client, relay and proof generator (containment list in research/privacy-successor-20261021/DESIGN.txt).
  b. Export rows (mint, P, amount) for every unconsumed lot at H, including lots stranded by an ordinary XMR spend (rights policy of the
     successor design: restore all unconsumed token-ledger rights; reviewed as a policy extension). Reconcile against public data with
     migration/snapshot-reconcile.mjs (catalog + public API; reproducible digest). Publish the snapshot digest and the registry id.
  c. Reconcile the custody reserve against the five buckets BEFORE enabling claims; a deposit awaiting credit must not be credited
     twice (old ledger and new). Record the reconciliation; refuse to activate on a mismatch.
  d. Enable claims in the wallet release (the Legacy rows). Keep claims open without an arbitrary expiry; late claims after the cutoff
     are still valid for lots in the snapshot; lots created after H are not in the snapshot and cannot claim (tested: unknown lot refused).
  e. Rollbacks around H: a reorganisation through H requires suspending claims and recomputing the snapshot against the new canonical
     chain (the release candidate's cutover-rollback test shows the boundary behaviour on the isolated legacy indexer).
6. NOT DONE HERE
  No production snapshot, no cutoff, no containment activation, no reserve reconciliation against the deployed custody, no migration of
  real EXTREMIST or any real holding. The real-mint decimals/catalog data are recorded (migration/third-party-assets.json) and compared,
  never acted on.
