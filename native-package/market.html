How settlement works
  1. The seller publishes a listing for one confirmed token lot.
  2. Buyer and seller review the same Monero transaction: tokens to the buyer and XMR to the seller.
  3. Both wallets approve it. After publication, the wallets verify ten Monero confirmations before the new token balance is spendable.

Buyers take the whole lot at the listed price. Both wallets must participate. No Solana wallet or link is needed.
